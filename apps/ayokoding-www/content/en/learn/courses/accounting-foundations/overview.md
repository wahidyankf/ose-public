## Prerequisites

- **Prior topics**: none.
- **Assumed knowledge**: signed arithmetic and careful reading of evidence.

## Why this exists

Accounting turns events into claims that other people can inspect. Start with the accounting equation, dual effects, and evidence before designing accounts or preparing statements. A ledger that balances but cannot explain its entries is not yet reliable.

## Scope boundary

This course establishes the ledger vocabulary. [Chart of Accounts and Data Modeling](../chart-of-accounts-and-data-modeling/overview.md) owns account structure; it does not appear here.
