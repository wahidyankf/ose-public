## Prerequisites

- **Prior topics**: [Accounting Foundations](../accounting-foundations/overview.md) and [SQL Essentials](../sql-essentials/learning/overview.md).
- **Assumed knowledge**: the accounting equation and basic record concepts.

## Why this exists

A chart of accounts makes balances useful for decisions and controls. You will create an original structure for a small entity, explain each grouping, and govern changes instead of copying a vendor or published chart.

## Scope boundary

This course links to `sql-essentials` for relational modeling and query skills. It does not re-teach SQL, relational design, or query performance.

## References

- A chart of accounts is organization-specific; example account names in this course are original.
