## Prerequisites

- **Prior topics**: [Chart of Accounts and Data Modeling](../chart-of-accounts-and-data-modeling/overview.md).
- **Assumed knowledge**: account classes and normal balances.

## Why this exists

A repeatable close converts classified balances into explainable statements. You will reconcile evidence, prepare draft statements, and identify changes that need review before a period is presented as final.

## Scope boundary

This course assembles and reviews reporting. [Journal Entries and Posting Mechanics](../journal-entries-and-posting-mechanics/overview.md) owns the detailed creation and control of journal entries.

## References

- Financial statements summarize classified balances over a period or at a reporting date.
