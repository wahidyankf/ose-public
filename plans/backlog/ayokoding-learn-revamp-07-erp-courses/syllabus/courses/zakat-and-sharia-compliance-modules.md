# Zakat and Sharia Compliance Modules (Annotated-Concept)

**Course ID**: `zakat-and-sharia-compliance-modules` · **Format**: Annotated-Concept.

**Scope note**: Designs a zakat module as governed calculation: base method, nisab, haul, rate, jurisdiction policy, dated parameters, approval, reproducible disclosure, and rollback, plus compliance modules built on the same discipline. It does not decide who owes zakat or what is zakatable for any person; it never issues a ruling.

**Short summary**: Compliance modules need governed inputs, a reviewable calculation policy, and a disclosure that can be reproduced.

## Why this exists · the big idea

- **The problem before the solution**: A zakat figure lives in a spreadsheet with a hidden price date and an unrecorded method, so nobody can reproduce or defend it.
- **Keep-this-if-you-forget-everything**: Record the method, the parameters with their dates, the approver, and the result so the figure can be rerun.

## Learning objectives

After this course you can:

1. compute a zakat base under the net assets method and under the net invested funds method.
2. apply nisab, haul date, and rate as dated, versioned parameters.
3. switch between jurisdiction policy profiles and compare the results.
4. record approval and produce a reproducible disclosure with a stable hash.
5. roll parameters back safely and flag the points a Sharia board or authority must decide.

## Accuracy notes

Provenance to cite in the course `## References` section. The maker re-verifies each item and the Content Quality Gate checks it; no inline confidence label is used.

- AAOIFI FAS 39 Zakah (effective 1 January 2023, supersedes FAS 9) and Sharia Standard 35 Zakah; AAOIFI describes the net assets method and the net invested funds method as base methods.
- Indonesia: Minister of Religious Affairs Regulation (PMA) 52/2014 articles 11 to 13 (business zakat: nisab equal to 85 g of gold, 2.5 percent, base current assets less short-term liabilities at the haul date, payment through an official amil) and PMA 31/2019 (income and services); verify the article text before quoting.
- BAZNAS publishes a 2.5 percent Hijri rate and a 2.575 percent Gregorian rate; its decree SK 15/2026 states an annual income nisab of Rp 91,681,728 priced at 14-carat gold, while a corporate page says pure gold. Present both and flag the inconsistency; the solar-year rate is derived as 2.5 x 365/354, about 2.577 percent.
- MUI Fatwa 3/2003 on zakat of income; PSAK 409 (Zakat dan Infak/Sedekah), renumbered from the old PSAK 109.

## Concepts

- **co-01 · zakat-base-method** — net assets or net invested funds as the starting base.
- **co-02 · nisab** — the minimum wealth threshold, priced in a stated way.
- **co-03 · hawl** — the lunar-year holding period and the haul date snapshot.
- **co-04 · rate-by-calendar** — 2.5 percent on a Hijri year and the adjusted rate on a solar year.
- **co-05 · jurisdiction-policy** — a country's rules, such as Indonesian ministerial and BAZNAS rules.
- **co-06 · amil-payment-route** — paying through an official collector where the jurisdiction requires it.
- **co-07 · input-governance** — who may supply and change calculation inputs.
- **co-08 · calculation-versioning** — freezing a calculation version for reruns.
- **co-09 · reproducible-disclosure** — a disclosure that can be regenerated byte for byte.
- **co-10 · approval-workflow** — preparer, reviewer, and approver for a calculation.
- **co-11 · effective-dated-parameters** — nisab price, rate, and policy values that change over time.
- **co-12 · non-permissible-income-flag** — a board decision point, not a calculation.
- **co-13 · audit-evidence** — inputs, parameters, approvals, and output kept together.
- **co-14 · parameter-rollback** — restoring earlier parameters while keeping the audit trail.
- **co-15 · policy-comparison-report** — the same entity computed under two policies side by side.
- **co-16 · non-ruling-boundary** — what the module and this course will not decide.
