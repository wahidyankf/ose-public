# Business Requirements — Audit of Computer Science, Systems, and Data Courses

## Problem

AyoKoding (`apps/ayokoding-www`) has 34 courses on computer science, systems and networking, databases and data, and
architecture and distributed systems that readers can open today. They are not outlines, and every one of them sits in
the three software-engineer learning paths. A reader who follows a path meets them as real steps, but they do not meet
the series definition of done:

- **Many are short of their floor, and some are thin enough to need authoring.** 14 of the 34 are below the word floor
  of their mode, by 257,144 words in all. `modern-system-programming` has 2,211 words for a By Example course that
  needs 28,000, `build-your-own-raft` has 2,578, and `build-your-own-database` has 2,755. 14 courses need 3,000 or
  more words written (270,187 words in all). 17 courses have a drilling page under 5,000
  words, 18 are missing at least one of the five drilling sections, and `capstone-solid-core` has no drilling page.
  About 14 to 16 of the 34 courses therefore need real authoring, not repair.
- **No example is proven to run.** None of the 34 courses has a `run.yaml`, so the code harness (plan 05) checks
  nothing in them. 112 kata folders exist where the floors ask for 254.
- **Lessons and files disagree.** 634 anchors do not match the file they point at (473 differ, 161 point at no
  file), and 1,110 code fences and 2,345 `Output` blocks are not tied to a file at all. A reader who copies a
  lesson's code may get a program that differs from the one the course says it ran.
- **Some examples cannot run as written.** `networking-essentials` and `advanced-networking` hold 99 uses of a real
  network; `computer-architecture` reads a clock 48 times in 80 examples; concurrency, distributed, and database
  courses depend on thread order, timers, and random failure. Their output cannot be recorded or trusted until each
  example is a deterministic loopback, fixture, model, or seeded simulation.
- **Six courses are templated filler.** Plan 09's filler guard flags `build-your-own-database`,
  `build-your-own-raft`, `csp-style-concurrency`, `linux-os`, `system-programming`, and `windows-os`, and tags them for
  this plan to fix.
- **One capstone has the wrong shape.** `capstone-solid-core` is one 15,009-word page with 35 files in a root `code/`
  folder, and another capstone (`capstone-real-world-delivery`, plan 08) leans on it.

## Who This Is For

- **Readers:** software engineers who learn computer science, systems, databases, or architecture on AyoKoding and
  expect each course to be finished: accurate text, examples they can run, and drilling to test themselves. Most
  arrive through a software-engineer learning path.
- **Authors and maintainers:** people who edit a course after merge. They need a test that stops a finished course from
  becoming thin again and a harness that tells them when an example stops running.
- **Later plans:** plan 13 audits the remaining pre-existing courses with the same method, the same registry, and the
  same CI decisions; plan 14 proves the series end state.
- **The PR reviewer:** one person reviews a very large PR and needs a commit per course and a record of each course's
  gates.
