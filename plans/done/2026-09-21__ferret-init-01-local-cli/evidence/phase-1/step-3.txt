## Method
Three passes per the workflow, because a rule restated in other words defeats a single-term search, plus whole-item accumulation
because a wrapped list item defeats a line-oriented one:
1. Subject terms: python, pytest, pytest-bdd, .pyz, zipapp, uv, `lang:` vocabulary, `cli-e2e`, harness-compatibility-protocol,
   generated skills routes.
2. Obligation verbs/nouns: mandatory target, install, static coverage, line coverage threshold, tag, register, mirror, generate.
3. Named surfaces: the Step 2 hits compared exactly with AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, repo-governance/development/infra/nx-targets/,
   docs/reference/platform-bindings.md, plus the surfaces those pointed to.
Whole-item accumulation: `rg -U` multi-line searches for "no Python", "Python application/app/project/library",
"Python (ruff)/only", and ".py ... format/corpora" so a statement wrapped across lines is not missed. The Step 2 hit list
(289 lines, 109 files) was read by area rather than sampled: nx-targets, behaviour-driven-development, ci-conventions,
harness workflows, quality/, and the two skills.
