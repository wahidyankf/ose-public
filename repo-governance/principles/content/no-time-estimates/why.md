# Why

## Why Plan Documents Carry No Estimates

1. **Plans Become Commitments**: A plan is the durable record executors follow. A duration written
   there reads as a commitment long after the guess behind it is forgotten
2. **Executors Vary Widely**: The same phase can be run by different people and agents at very
   different speeds. No single figure is true for all of them
3. **Estimates Go Stale**: A plan waits in `ideas/` or `backlog/` before it starts, so any duration
   in it is out of date by execution time
4. **Dependency Order Is Checkable**: Whether a step blocks another can be verified; whether it
   takes "two days" cannot. Plans stay executable when they state what can be checked

## Why Estimates Are Permitted Elsewhere

1. **Estimates Inform Decisions**: In conversation and status updates, an estimate helps the owner
   decide what to run next and how to spend resources
2. **Records Stay Honest**: Evidence and `learnings.md` record what actually happened, including how
   long it took, so later plans learn from it
3. **Readers Prepare**: A tutorial or guide may tell readers roughly what to set aside
4. **The Label Carries the Caveat**: Marking the figure as an estimate keeps a reader from mistaking
   it for a measurement or a promise
