# Relationship to Other Principles

- [Explicit Over Implicit](../explicit-over-implicit.md) - Descriptive errors and exact names are
  explicitness applied to consumer surfaces; this principle extends it with the no-silence demand.
- [Simplicity Over Complexity](../../general/simplicity-over-complexity.md) - When terse and
  descriptive conflict, descriptive wins. Simplicity here means easy to use correctly, never
  fewer words at the cost of silence.
- [Accessibility First](../../content/accessibility-first.md) - A consumer who can tell what went
  wrong and what to do next is included; one left guessing is not.
