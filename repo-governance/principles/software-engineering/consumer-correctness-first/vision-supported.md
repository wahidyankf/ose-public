# Vision Supported

The [Vision](../../../vision/open-sharia-enterprise.md) is making trustworthy, Shariah-compliant
enterprise systems more accessible. Accessibility fails when a surface lets misuse pass quietly:
a newcomer who cannot tell what went wrong, or what a name means, is locked out regardless of
documentation. Consumer-correct surfaces — descriptive errors, exact names, no silence — lower
that barrier, so a global contributor can use the platform correctly on the first try.
